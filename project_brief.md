# My agent: Multi-Sport Game Video Analyst & Coach

One-liner: A conversational sports analysis agent that helps athletes and coaches analyze gameplay recordings (Cricket, Badminton, Hockey, etc.) by identifying the sport, breaking down what went well vs. what went wrong, and recommending tailored improvement drills and strategies.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers player skill level, past match reports, recurring weaknesses (e.g. footwork issues, grip, shot selection), and historical progress across sessions.
- Tools: Video analysis/frame inspection lookup, drill & playbook retrieval tool, match report generator.
- Catalog/UI: Drill & strategy playbook catalog, match summary scorecards, and swing/technique breakdown rendered as A2UI cards and tables.
- Image gen: Generates annotated tactical diagrams, court/pitch positioning visuals, or ideal stance illustrations.
- Sandbox: Computes performance statistics, strike rates, shot distributions, and error percentages.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Multimodal video processing (Gemini video understanding/Omni), Code execution sandbox for calculating match metrics, Cloud Trace for latency tracking.
